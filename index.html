Lilac Valley Farm Stay

"A stay free from distractions"

Lilac Valley Farm Stay, owned by interior designer Marina Ye, is a luxurious rustic retreat in the Blue Mountains. Nestled on four acres with sheep, chickens, ducks, and a friendly goat named Rosie, it offers a peaceful escape.

About

Lilac Valley Farm Stay offers a range of thoughtful inclusions for a relaxing stay. Guests can enjoy fresh farm eggs, a fully equipped kitchen, and complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the property. The cozy fireplace adds warmth to the open-plan living space, while the outdoor plunge pool and beautiful garden provide perfect spots to unwind. Eco-friendly toiletries, luxury linens, and plush towels ensure a comfortable experience, making this a truly restful getaway in a picturesque setting.
